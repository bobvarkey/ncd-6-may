# ESC STAMP on CKD - Clinical Decision Tool

## Overview
The ESC "STAMP on CKD" infographic is a clinical decision tool for managing cardiovascular disease (CVD) patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD).

## STAMP Acronym

### S – Screen for CKD
- Test **eGFR** (estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate) and **uACR** (urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio) in all CVD patients at diagnosis
- Then test at least **annually** in those with:
  - Diabetes
  - High risk of worsening kidney function

### T – Triage and Stage CKD
- **Diagnose CKD** if:
  - eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m², OR
  - uACR ≥3 mg/mmol
  - Persistent for ≥3 months
- Use **KDIGO heatmap** for staging:
  - 🟢 Green (Low risk)
  - 🟡 Yellow (Moderately increased risk)
  - 🟠 Orange (High risk)
  - 🔴 Red (Very high risk)

### A – Assess CVD Risk
- Evaluate cardiovascular risk in CKD patients
- Consider renal function in CVD risk stratification

### M – Manage CKD and CVD Together
- Integrated management approach
- Consider kidney function when prescribing cardiovascular medications
- Adjust doses based on eGFR

### P – Prevent Cardiovascular Events
- Primary and secondary prevention strategies
- Optimize risk factors
- Consider cardiorenal syndrome implications

## Key Tests
| Test | Abbreviation | Purpose |
|------|--------------|---------|
| Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate | eGFR | Measure kidney function |
| Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio | uACR | Detect protein in urine (albuminuria) |

## Clinical Pearls
- CKD and CVD often coexist (cardiorenal syndrome)
- KDIGO heatmap guides management decisions
- Regular monitoring is essential for high-risk patients
- Drug dosing adjustments needed based on renal function
